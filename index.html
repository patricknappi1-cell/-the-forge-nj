WHY THE FORGE EXISTS

WE ARE NOT HERE TO CREATE SPECTATORS.

The Forge exists to call people into a deeper, obedient walk with Jesus Christ.

We believe biblical Christianity is not merely something to agree with. It is a life to surrender to and walk out. The Forge brings together Scripture, prayer, worship, physical discipline, confession, repentance, brotherhood, leadership and service.

The goal is not hype. The goal is transformation.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

TRUTH BEFORE TREND.

Our foundation is Jesus Christ and the written Word of God.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for faith, doctrine and Christian living.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, fully God and fully man, who died for our sins, rose from the dead and is Lord.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by the grace of God through faith in Jesus Christ, producing repentance, a changed life and obedience.

The Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit empowers believers to live holy lives, bear witness to Christ and serve with spiritual power.

Discipleship

We believe followers of Jesus are called to grow into maturity, deny themselves, carry their cross, obey Christ and make disciples.
