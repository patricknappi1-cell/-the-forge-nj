DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT IN THE APP

You can permanently delete your The Forge account directly inside the app.

  1. Sign in to The Forge.
  2. Open Chat and go to My Profile.
  3. Scroll to Privacy & Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

WHAT IS DELETED

When you permanently delete your account, The Forge removes account data associated with your membership, including:

  • Your sign-in/authentication account
  • Your member profile, display name, optional bio, and profile photo
  • Your channel chat messages and private/direct-message history
  • Photos and videos you uploaded to Forge chat
  • Your notification preferences and registered push-notification devices
  • Membership, blocking, and other account records tied to your profile

IF YOU CANNOT ACCESS THE APP

If you are unable to sign in and need help deleting your account, email us from the email address associated with your Forge account at info@theforgenj.com with the subject Delete My Forge Account.

We may need to verify that you own the account before completing a deletion request.

Limited technical records. Active Forge account/profile content is not kept after account deletion. Limited security logs or backup copies may remain temporarily under our service providers' normal security and backup processes and are not used to recreate your deleted account.

QUESTIONS

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact info@theforgenj.com.
